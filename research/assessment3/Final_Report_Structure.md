# MSE907 Assessment 3 – Final Research Report Structure

Assessment 3 requires the Final Research Project Report and presentation. The official brief assesses literature/gap analysis (LO1), the research-informed report (LO3), ethical/cultural issues (LO4), and presentation/communication (LO5).

## Chapter 1 – Introduction
- Background
- Problem statement
- Research gap
- Aim
- RQs
- Objectives
- Scope
- Significance

## Chapter 2 – Literature Review
- Blockchain in pharmaceutical traceability
- Interoperability
- Hybrid on/off-chain architecture
- Privacy/security
- Evaluation practices
- Adoption research
- Critical synthesis and final research gap

## Chapter 3 – Research Methodology
- DSR
- Peffers process
- Mixed methods
- SLR/PRISMA
- Technical experiments
- Stakeholder/TAM evaluation
- Ethical procedure

## Chapter 4 – Requirements and System Design
- Functional/non-functional requirements
- Architecture
- User workflow
- Sequence diagrams
- Flowcharts
- Interoperability schema
- Data model
- Threat/security model

## Chapter 5 – Implementation
- Hardhat local EVM network
- Chaincode
- Backend
- Interoperability layer
- PostgreSQL
- IPFS/off-chain storage
- React dashboards
- Authentication/RBAC
- Privacy mechanisms
- Cross-ledger adapter

## Chapter 6 – Testing and Evaluation
- Functional testing
- Performance/TPS/latency
- Scalability
- Interoperability
- Integrity/hash tests
- Authorization/security tests
- Privacy/ZK proof demonstration
- TAM/user evaluation if approved and collected

## Chapter 7 – Discussion
- Findings mapped to RQs
- Findings mapped to objectives
- Comparison with literature
- Software-engineering contribution
- Industry implications
- Limitations

## Chapter 8 – Ethical and Cultural Discussion
- Privacy
- Consent
- Data governance
- Bias
- Accessibility
- Cultural considerations
- Implications for industry

## Chapter 9 – Conclusion and Future Work
- Contributions
- Conclusions
- Limitations
- Future research
