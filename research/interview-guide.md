# Stakeholder Interview Guide

Use only after ethics approval.

1. Describe your current pharmaceutical traceability process.
2. Where do you experience delays, fragmented information or verification problems?
3. What information must be shared with other organizations?
4. Which information should remain confidential?
5. What interoperability barriers exist between current systems?
6. What implementation costs, skills or organizational barriers do you expect?
7. What would make a blockchain-based traceability system trustworthy and usable?
8. Which performance or operational indicators matter most?
9. How should regulatory/compliance information be handled?
10. What cultural, accessibility or organizational considerations should the system address?
